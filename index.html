FAQ

Good questions

Do you provide the alcohol?

Usually you supply the alcohol and we provide the bartenders, tools, and know-how. We'll send a shopping list based on your guest count and menu so you don't overbuy or run out.

Can I book just the DJ or just the bartender?

Yes. Both services are available on their own, but booking them together saves you money and simplifies planning.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, 3 to 6 months ahead is ideal, especially for Saturdays in peak season. For smaller events, a few weeks is often enough. Reach out and we'll check your date.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Our bartenders are certified in responsible alcohol service and we carry liability insurance. We can provide a certificate of insurance to your venue.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in [Your City] and serve the surrounding area. Travel outside our main area may include a small fee.

How do deposits work?

A deposit holds your date, and the remaining balance is due before the event. Details are in your quote.
